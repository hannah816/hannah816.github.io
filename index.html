01 / Urban climate

Micro-Urban Heat Island Effects of Surface Parking Lots

How do parking-lot design and the surrounding landscape influence extreme surface temperatures? I built and validated a geospatial inventory of more than 2,000 open-air parking lots, then combined Landsat, LiDAR, land-cover, and urban-form data to identify the strongest environmental drivers of heat.

  • Google Earth Engine
  • Python
  • Machine learning
  • SHAP
  • LiDAR

About

Connecting environmental processes to human decisions.

I’m completing a B.S. in Earth and Environmental Sciences and a B.A. in Climate Studies, with a minor in Data Science, at Vanderbilt University. My work moves between urban heat, regional climate, water quality, and resource recovery—but the thread is consistent: using spatial evidence to understand environmental change and support practical action.

I’m especially interested in land–atmosphere interactions, environmental AI, multimodal remote sensing, and interpretable models. For graduate study, I hope to develop deeper computational methods while keeping the questions grounded in climate adaptation and sustainability.
